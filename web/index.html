A workbench for engineering decisions.

  1. Explore signals. Add up to three traces. Use raw values for one signal or standardized overlays for comparison.
  2. Choose a window. Show 50, 100, or all records. Scrub the range and click a point or fingerprint column.
  3. Compare evidence. Pin two or three entities and compare measurements and test outcomes.
  4. Work the queue. Filter by reason or owner, inspect a case, and save a status and next verification step.
  5. Control replay. Adjust records per tick, pause, or start a new warehouse. Previous warehouses are preserved.
